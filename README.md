# oneglassOS
One Glass OS
​One Glass OS is a modern, web-based operating system designed with a translucent frosted-glass theme. It provides a clean, distraction-free digital workspace equipped with productivity tools, customized applications, and desktop widgets directly inside your web browser.
​What Built-In Applications Are Included?
​1. Glass Terminal (Advanced App)
​A command-line terminal with a special dual-tab interface in the title bar:
​Terminal Tab: Allows users to type and run interactive commands.
​Commands Tab: A built-in menu that displays all available system commands so first-time users can easily learn how to navigate the OS.
​Available Commands:
​help - Lists all system commands.
​clear - Clears the terminal screen.
​time - Shows the current system time and date.
​open [app] - Launches desktop applications (e.g., open focus, open weather, open settings).
​echo [text] - Prints custom text to the screen.
​theme [dark/glass] - Toggles a dark filter over the desktop background.
​about - Displays OS system details.
​2. Glass Focus Suite
​A productivity app designed to keep you focused on work or study:
​Pomodoro Timer: A 25-minute countdown timer with Start, Pause, and Reset controls.
​Focus Goals: A checklist to add daily tasks and cross them off when complete.
​Widget Hub: A central panel to toggle persistent desktop widgets on or off.
​3. Weather Glass
​A live weather forecast application:
​Search Bar: Look up weather details for any city around the world.
​Metrics: Displays local temperature, sky conditions, humidity levels, and wind speed.
​4. System Settings
​A system control window:
​Master Volume: Adjust system audio percentage.
​Screen Dimmer: Drag a slider to physically dim or brighten your desktop view.
​5. Desktop Widgets & Dock
​Frosted Flip Clock: Displays a digital clock on your desktop.
​Mini Calendar: Displays today's day, date, and month.
​Status Bar Dock: A floating dock at the bottom of the screen for quick one-click app access.
​How to Use One Glass OS
​Enter the Desktop: Click the "Launch" button or the "X" button on the Welcome screen to dismiss the intro overlay and reveal your desktop.
​Open Applications: Click any icon on the desktop or click any app slot on the bottom dock.
​Move Windows: Click and drag the top title bar of any window or widget to move it anywhere on your screen.
​Use Fullscreen: Click the fullscreen symbol (⤢) in the top-right corner of a window to expand it.
​Explore the Terminal: Open Glass Terminal, click the "Commands" tab in the title bar to see what commands you can run, and then switch back to the "Terminal" tab to try typing them out!
